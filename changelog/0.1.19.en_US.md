# The engine learned to draw someone as a ghost

An engine release for Combat 0.1.11: the bridge got the path a mod needs to ask for someone to be drawn as the translucent silhouette the game already uses for players who are invisible and on your team. On its own it changes nothing — what turns it on is the Combat option.

- **It is the game's own ghost**: the two points where `RendererLivingEntity` decides between drawing normally, not drawing, and drawing at 15% now ask the mods, and only inside that method. The drawing is the game's, with the game's code.
- **No armour on top**: in the game, armour and the held item are drawn outside of invisibility — that is why an invisible player in armour looks like walking armour. On a ghost they are left out, otherwise the silhouette would hide everything behind it.
- **And nothing else that comes outside the body**: the flames of a burning player and the potion particles go away on a ghost too. The game already spawns fewer particles for invisible players, but "fewer" still marks where someone is; here it is none. They keep burning and keep their potions for everything else — it is only the screen.
- **Nor the red of damage**: the colour the game paints over the whole model when it takes a hit (and the creeper white, and the death fade) does not happen on a ghost.
- **Nothing changes for anyone who leaves the option off**: the game draws exactly what it drew before.
