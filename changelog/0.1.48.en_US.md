# Stein Loader 0.1.48: the Sprint mod and motion blur

Toggle sprint gets a mod of its own that comes with the Stein Client, with your movement state on the HUD, and the video settings gain motion blur and a switch for chunks fading in.

## Sprint

- **Sprint comes with the Stein Client**: toggle sprint (G) and toggle sneak with a key. The mod holds the game's key for you, so the game still decides when you sprint (forward only, hunger, items in use) and the server sees exactly what it would see from a player holding the key.
- **Your movement state on the HUD**: `[Sprinting (Toggled)]`, `[Sneaking (Key Held)]`, flying, descending and riding, in three text styles. By default it shows only what is happening; it can also show the toggle while you stand still.
- **More options**: turn off sprinting by double-tapping forward, stop sneaking when you start flying, and let the game's own sprint and sneak keys toggle too. Servers can turn either toggle off.
- **Your old key comes along**: toggle sprint used to live in Combat. On the first start, Sprint brings over whether it was on and the key you had chosen.

## Graphics

- **Motion blur** (Video settings > Camera): Motion blurs the picture along the way the camera moves, the same at any frame rate; Trail leaves a fading trail of the last frames. Strength from 1 to 10. The HUD, the crosshair and your hand stay sharp, and nothing is blurred while the camera is still. Off by default.
- **Chunks fading in can be turned off** (Video settings > World): off, loaded chunks appear at once instead of coming out of the fog.

## Menu

- **Client mods first**: the Mods list and the Panel show the mods that come with the Stein Client (HUD, Connect, Replay, Sprint) before the extra ones.

## For mod makers

- **Loader API 0.1.35**: `Entities.isSprinting`, `isSneaking`, `isRiding` and `isFlying`; `Gameplay.setDoubleTapSprint`; `SteinMod.onEntityColor` to tint a living entity like the damage red; the `run` icon for Panel pages.
