# Stein Loader opens up to mods by other authors (experimental API)

For players, nothing changes on screen: the game and its performance are the same as 0.1.21. The news is underneath — Stein Loader now has what a mod by another author needs, and the **Stein SDK**, the kit to write those mods, comes out with it.

- **Experimental API**: inventory (moving items, putting armour on and off, with the inventory open or closed), aiming and attacks, entity damage and health, plus what was already there (HUD, Panel, chat, sounds, networking). Experimental means it may still change in the next versions.
- **On the Panel**, mods get sliders and key pickers in their options.
- **Mods built with the SDK** are Java 25, like the game, and go into the `mods` folder like any `.steinmod`.

To update, open the installer again. Mods, options and worlds stay where they are.
