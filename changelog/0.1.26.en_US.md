# Stein Loader 0.1.26: keys in the Panel and groundwork for the new Combat

Panel tweaks and what Combat 0.1.16 needs to work.

- **Keys in the Panel**: a feature can have its key on its own row, in a small square next to the switch. Click it and press the key; right-click clears it.
- **Stein menu**: the side tab no longer flickers when it closes.
- **Options that show up only when they apply**: the Panel hides options that depend on another one that is off.
- **Very transparent backgrounds** in the Panel no longer disappear.
- **Components**: Stein Bridge 0.1.15 and Stein Loader 0.1.19.

To update, open the Stein Client or the installer again. Mods, options and worlds stay where they are.
