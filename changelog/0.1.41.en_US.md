# Stein Loader 0.1.41: Stein's loading screen is back

The game opens on Stein's own loading screen again, and the Stein logo is back in the inventory's corner.

- **Stein's loading screen**: the dark Stein screen with a progress bar replaces the old Minecraft splash when the game opens and whenever resources reload (a texture pack, the language, the mipmap levels), then fades into the game. It works on Vulkan and OpenGL; on the old graphics path you keep the game's own screen.
- **Stein logo in the inventory**: a small Stein mark in the top right corner of the inventory and of the creative inventory.
- **Components**: Stein Bridge 0.1.27 and Stein Loader 0.1.30.

To update, open the Stein Client or the installer again. Mods, options and worlds stay where they are.
