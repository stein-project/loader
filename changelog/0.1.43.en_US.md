# Stein Loader 0.1.43: fainter ghosts

Players that a mod turns into ghosts are more transparent now, so it is easier to see who is fighting behind them.

- **Fainter ghosts**: a mod's ghost (like the hidden clan and the ghost allies in Combat) is drawn at about half the strength of before. Players the game itself makes see-through, like an invisible teammate, look the same as always.
- **Components**: Stein Bridge 0.1.28 and Stein Loader 0.1.31.

To update, open the Stein Client or the installer again. Mods, options and worlds stay where they are.
