# Stein Loader 0.1.38: the inventory back on older graphics drivers

A fix for players whose graphics card has an older driver, like many Intel laptops.

- **The whole inventory again**: with Vulkan, some older drivers did not show the items or your character in the inventory. On those computers the game now opens with OpenGL, and everything shows.
- **Automatic switch**: when Vulkan cannot run on your computer, the Stein Client switches to OpenGL by itself and tells you why. If you update your graphics driver, you can choose Vulkan again in Settings.
- **Components**: Stein Bridge 0.1.24 and Stein Loader 0.1.29.

To update, open the Stein Client or the installer again. Mods, options and worlds stay where they are.
