# Stein Loader 0.1.46: install into Prism Launcher

The installer can now also put Stein into Prism Launcher, as an instance of its own, without touching your others.

- **Prism Launcher**: in the installer's folder list, pick Prism's (it shows as "Prism Launcher"). It creates the instance "Stein Loader 1.8.9" with the Java 25, the memory you chose and the mods. It works on Windows, Linux (Flatpak included) and macOS.
- **Close Prism before installing**: with it open, the new instance's settings may be undone. Then open Prism and play the instance.
- **Components**: Stein Bridge 0.1.29 and Stein Loader 0.1.33, the same as the previous pack.

To update, open the Stein Client or the installer again. Mods, options and worlds stay where they are.
