# Stein Loader 0.1.32: the Loader does not favour any mod

The Loader no longer keeps a part reserved for one mod: the sprint and hit switches that only Combat could reach are now in the public mod API.

- **Gameplay switches in the API**: sprint in all directions, no hit delay, sprint through the hit and no hit slowdown are now open to any mod (`Gameplay`, in the Stein SDK 0.2.4). Servers detect them, so a mod that offers them should warn the player and start them off.
- **Mods built before keep working**: Combat 0.1.20 and older still load and work the same.
- **Components**: Stein Bridge 0.1.20 and Stein Loader 0.1.25.

To update, open the Stein Client or the installer again. Mods, options and worlds stay where they are.
