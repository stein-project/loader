# Stein Loader 0.1.31: mods from their own indexes

The official mods now come from each mod's own index, and Combat and Mouse leave the official list.

- **Mods from their own indexes**: every official mod publishes its own list of versions (steinmods.json) with each release, and the installer reads it from there. A new version of a mod reaches you as soon as it is released.
- **Combat and Mouse are third-party mods**: they are open source now and are released by their author, outside the official list. Whoever already has them keeps them: the installer never removes a .steinmod it does not manage, and the Stein Client keeps updating them.
- **Components**: Stein Bridge 0.1.20 and Stein Loader 0.1.24.

To update, open the Stein Client or the installer again. Mods, options and worlds stay where they are.
