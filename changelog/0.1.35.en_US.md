# Stein Loader 0.1.35: safer logs and server resource packs

This version closes two ways a server could reach into your game or learn about your computer.

- **Log4Shell closed in every launcher**: Minecraft 1.8.9 still carries an old log4j, and only the official launcher passed Mojang's fix. A chat message with `${...}` could be expanded in the log or make the game connect to an address the server chose. The Bridge now turns that off before the game starts, whatever the launcher (Stein Client, official or older ones).
- **Resource packs can no longer look at your files**: a server could ask for a `level://` pack outside your worlds folder and learn whether any file exists on your computer. Only the pack of a world (`<world>/resources.zip`) is accepted now, as in later versions.
- **Your accounts stay apart**: the server pack cache was shared by every account, so a server could tell two accounts are on the same computer. A pack saved by one account is now downloaded again by the others.
- **No packs from your own network**: a server could point a pack at your router or a service on your computer to find out whether it is there. Those addresses are refused now, unless the server itself is on your computer or network (LAN).
- **Components**: Stein Bridge 0.1.21 and Stein Loader 0.1.27.

To update, open the Stein Client or the installer again. Mods, options and worlds stay where they are.
