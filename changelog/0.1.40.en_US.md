# Stein Loader 0.1.40: more FPS in big fights, steadier frames

Crowded scenes run faster and the frame rate dips less: in our tests a 400-player fight went from about 730 to 900 FPS with Vulkan and its worst frames got lighter. The built-in benchmark now films exactly the same scene in every graphics mode, so the result you send us is easy to compare.

- **More FPS with many players**: players and their armor are drawn in far fewer batches, and the game allocates much less memory every frame. In our tests heavy scenes gained 13% to 45% with Vulkan and 4% to 17% with OpenGL.
- **Fewer stutters**: the game no longer waits for the singleplayer server in the middle of a frame, and animated textures no longer interrupt the frame on Vulkan.
- **Steadier frames in fights and over new land**: each game tick is 15% to 20% lighter in big fights, and loading new land creates much less garbage. In our tests the 1% low went up 6% to 19%.
- **Java memory tuned**: Java now keeps about 1 GB before cleaning up, instead of 768 MB, so it cleans far less often. In our tests the 1% low went up 12% to 50%, for about 180 MB more memory. The memory cap does not change.
- **Vulkan stays on**: running the benchmark could switch the game to OpenGL as if Vulkan had crashed. If that happened to you, turn Vulkan back on in the video settings.
- **No more empty world after a long teleport**: in singleplayer, a long `/tp` could leave you in a world that never loaded until you changed dimension.
- **A benchmark you can trust**: every biome is checked under the camera, the setup takes about 17 seconds instead of over a minute, and parts that cannot be measured say why.
- **Components**: Stein Bridge 0.1.26 and Stein Loader 0.1.30.

To update, open the Stein Client or the installer again. Mods, options and worlds stay where they are.
