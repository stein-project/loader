# The engine learned to hide entities

An engine release: Stein Bridge got the hook a mod needs to take someone off the screen, which is what Combat 0.1.6 uses to hide allies. On its own it changes nothing you see — but the new Combat asks for it.

- **Every mod asks for this version**: Combat, HUD, Connect and Mouse now require Loader 0.1.9 in their manifest, so there is no version mix nobody tested — update the Loader before the mods.
- **New `onHideEntity` hook**: a mod can say an entity must not be drawn, and nothing of it shows up — body, armor, nametag and shadow, including in the shadow pass. It is not the game's invisibility, which would still show the armor.
- **New home for the downloads**: the installer and each mod now have their own repository under the project account. The in-game update notice keeps working as before for anyone who already has the Loader installed.
