# The engine learned to hold the sprint through a hit

An engine release for Combat 0.1.9: the bridge got the hook a mod needs to stop a hit from dropping your sprint. On its own it changes nothing — what turns it on is the Combat option, which starts off.

- **Only the call inside the hit**: the redirect is scoped to `attackTargetEntityWithCurrentItem`, and the build checks that it was a single point in the game. The calls that drop the sprint when you stop moving forward, sneak or touch a wall are untouched.
- **Nothing changes for anyone who leaves the option off**: the game does exactly what it did before.
