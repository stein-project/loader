# Stein Loader 0.1.36: players back on Intel graphics, and rules for each server

This version brings back players that disappeared on some computers and lets servers say which features they do not allow.

- **Players visible again on Intel graphics**: with Stein GFX on, some Intel drivers refused to copy the players' skins, and only the name and the item in the hand showed. The skins now go through another way when that happens.
- **Thick items with any resource pack**: some packs leave a nearly invisible background in their item pictures, and the item came out flat in your hand. Those items have their thickness again.
- **Text selection in the chat**: selecting text in the chat or in any text box shows the highlight again with Stein GFX on.
- **Rules for each server**: on a server that does not allow it, the HUD turns off the minimap, the target information and the distance in the Tab list, Keybind holds the chat macros and Replay waits for the end of the match to show your death. They come back as soon as you leave that server.
- **Broken or harmful mods stopped**: a mod known to be broken or harmful is no longer loaded, wherever it came from.
- **Signed information**: the latest version, the menu message, the official mods and the server rules now come from a signed source, and the game refuses anything changed on the way.
- **Logs by topic**: when someone asks you for a log to fix a problem, you can turn on and send just the part that matters.
- **Components**: Stein Bridge 0.1.22 and Stein Loader 0.1.28.

To update, open the Stein Client or the installer again. Mods, options and worlds stay where they are.
