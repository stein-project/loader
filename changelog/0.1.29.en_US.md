# Stein Loader 0.1.29: less load while the game is idle

The game stops wasting CPU and graphics card when nobody is playing, and an old mod no longer closes the game with Stein GFX.

- **Frame limit while idle**: out of focus (another window in front, or minimized) and on the title screen, 30 frames per second; on the Esc menu, 60. While playing nothing changes, and the limit you chose in the options still applies when it is lower.
- **Mods incompatible with Stein GFX**: with Stein GFX on, a mod that calls old OpenGL directly is left out and shows in the mod list as incompatible, with the reason, instead of closing the game. To use it, turn Stein GFX off in the Stein Client settings.
- **Components**: Stein Bridge 0.1.19 and Stein Loader 0.1.23.

To update, open the Stein Client or the installer again. Mods, options and worlds stay where they are.
