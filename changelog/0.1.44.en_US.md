# Stein Loader 0.1.44: Stein's video settings

The game's video settings make way for a new Stein screen, with every option explained in your language, new image options, choosing Vulkan or OpenGL in game, and custom skies from resource packs.

- **New video settings**: options are grouped by topic (General, Image, Display and HDR, World, Textures, Camera and players, 1.7 animations, Performance), each with a hint on what it does, the game's own options included. Sliders apply when you let go, and you can watch the change on your own view or on a landscape.
- **Image options**: edge smoothing (textures stay crisp), sharpness, texture filters and anisotropy.
- **Resolution**: draw the world at 30% to 200% of the screen size. Below 100% gives FPS on weaker graphics cards; above, a smoother image.
- **HDR** (Vulkan, on HDR monitors), with brightness calibration.
- **Vulkan or OpenGL in game**: the choice lives in the video settings and the Stein Client follows it.
- **Custom skies**: resource packs that bring their own sky now show it.
- **Damage camera shake**: now an option.
- **More FPS with many entities**: 14% to 18% more FPS where there are lots of animals or mobs, with the same image.
- **Names**: an empty name over a head no longer leaves a small black box.
- **The Loader's menus share the look** of the new video settings.
- **Components**: Stein Bridge 0.1.29 and Stein Loader 0.1.32.

To update, open the Stein Client or the installer again. Mods, options and worlds stay where they are.
