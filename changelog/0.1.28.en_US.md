# Stein Loader 0.1.28: 1.7 animations, Stein GFX and chunks fading in

The 1.7 animations arrive and, for whoever wants to try it, Stein's new renderer. The new look and the Stein loading screen are gone, and the game is back to the 1.8.9 textures.

- **Stein GFX (experimental)**: Stein's new renderer, on its own graphics API over modern OpenGL. Off by default; turn it on with `-Dsteinbridge.gfx=true` in the Java arguments. F3 shows which one is drawing (Stein GFX or Fixed-function pipeline) and the frame time.
- **Chunks fading in (Stein GFX)**: like in newer versions, a chunk that shows up on screen for the first time fades from the sky color into its own, instead of popping in. A rebuilt chunk (block broken, pack switch) does not fade again.
- **New look removed**: the textures and sounds of recent Minecraft are gone, along with the new art on HUD icons and CIT on the new art. The game, the HUD and your resource pack are back to how they were; the installer deletes the pack left in the folder.
- **Stein loading screen removed**: startup and reloads are back to the 1.8.9 screens.
- **Stein graphics effects removed**: shadows, water, plants and glowing blocks, dynamic lights, occlusion, sun rays, sky, rain and puddles, HDR and post-processing belonged to the old renderer and will come back later, rebuilt on Stein GFX. Connected textures, CIT, zoom, FOV and the performance options stay; the graphics selector keeps Fast and Fancy.
- **1.7 animations**: new topic in Video Settings, all on by default and on your screen only. Block hitting, 1.7 positions for held items, the blocking sword, bow and fishing rod, 1.7 third-person blocking and red armor on damage; each part can be turned on or off on its own. Works with both renderers.
- **Switching packs no longer breaks textures**: CIT armor no longer turns purple and black after switching packs and back, and with Stein GFX the ground no longer goes black and unlit after a switch.
- **Panel**: mouse buttons (side and middle) can be bound to any feature; screen contents no longer spill past the card edge with Stein GFX.
- **For modders**: `Server.latency` (the ping measured by the client itself, like the server list), `Inventory.resync`, `Render.playerSkin` (a player's skin by nick, like in the Tab) and `Hud.setHidden` (hide the whole HUD) and `Keys.click` (a click in the mouse queue, like the physical mouse).
- **New defaults**: a fresh install comes with a tidy HUD layout, and dynamic FOV, compact angles and direct rendering start off.
- **Components**: Stein Bridge 0.1.18 and Stein Loader 0.1.22.

To update, open the Stein Client or run the installer again. Mods, options and worlds stay where they are.
