# Stein Loader 0.1.47: a steadier game in crowded fights and dozens of fixes

This version fixes what made the game freeze for seconds in fights with many players, brings dozens of 1.8.9 bug fixes (many asked for by players and testers), protects the game from malicious servers and raises the frame limit to 3000.

## Stability

- **Fewer freezes in crowded fights**: the game no longer stops for seconds when the graphics card falls behind in fights with dozens of players, and it no longer reuses memory the card is still reading. Waits for the card now go in slices, and a long one leaves a line in the log saying what it was waiting for.
- **Frame watchdog**: if a frame takes longer than 2 seconds in a world, the log records where the game was stuck (`-Dsteinbridge.watchdog.frameMs`).
- **Graphics card memory**: on Vulkan, the log shows how much video memory the game uses and how much the system gives it, to find slowdowns when it runs out.
- **Item icons that disappeared**: after seeing many item variants (dyed armour, potions, detailed texture packs), new icons stopped showing until resources were reloaded. Now the models' room grows and starts over on its own.

## Logs

- **Session log always on**: everything the game prints (the game's log, the chat, the Bridge, the Loader and the mods) goes to `logs/stein/latest.log`, whatever launcher started it. On every start, the previous session goes into a `.zip` in the same folder (the 200 most recent are kept). Whoever freezes and opens the game again no longer loses the log that mattered.
- **Your account's token out of the logs**: the line where 1.8.9 wrote your account's access token now says `token:<hidden>`.
- **Cleaner logs**: repeated errors of game tasks show the full error once and then only how many times it repeated; the unknown sound warning appears once per sound; "Unable to locate sign" goes to the log instead of the chat.

## Gameplay and controls

- **No aim delay (MC-67665)**: the block or entity under the crosshair follows the camera at once, as from 1.11. In 1.8.9 it trailed a tick behind.
- **Hotbar keys on the mouse**: mouse buttons bound to the hotbar now move items inside chests and the inventory, like the keyboard's.
- **Block placing sound at once**, as from 1.9, without waiting for the server and without playing twice.
- **Keys held while closing a screen**: holding W (or another key) while closing the inventory or the chat works again right away, as from 1.12.
- **Keys typed in the same tick as a click** that changes the screen go to the new screen, and a character typed through an IME no longer takes a screenshot by itself.
- **AZERTY, QWERTZ and other keyboards**: letters and symbols follow your keyboard again, with the right names in the controls screen.

## Linux

- **"Open folder" for resource packs and chat links** open again: through `xdg-open` first (KDE, Wayland sessions) and off the game thread, without freezing the screen.

## Screens and options

- **Frame limit up to 3000**, in steps of 5 (it was 250, in steps of 10).
- **Mouse wheel on Stein's sliders**: with the mouse over any slider of the Bridge, the Loader or the HUD editor, the wheel changes one value at a time; the arrow keys and Shift while dragging give fine control.
- **Options saved when leaving with Esc**: field of view, sensitivity, brightness, render distance, chat and controls are no longer lost when closing the screen without clicking "Done".
- **`options.txt` is not lost** in a crash or a power cut: the file is written whole before it replaces the old one.
- **The game's mipmap slider** applies only on release, instead of reloading every texture at each step.
- **Death screen buttons** no longer stay disabled after resizing the window.
- **Potion levels above IV** show in the inventory and in the potion's tooltip.
- **Ghost click above the chat**: a click just above the visible lines no longer triggers links of hidden lines.
- **Resource packs taken off the list** let go of their `.zip` file, which can be moved or deleted.
- **Server list**: a corrupt server icon, an address made only of colons, an invalid address and spaces around the address no longer crash the screen or block the connection.

## Sound

- **Armour breaking**: in multiplayer you hear and see your own armour break, as from 1.15.
- **Pause**: when you come back from the pause menu, only the sounds the pause stopped play again, not the ones that had already ended.

## Picture

- **White block up high**: a block placed more than 12 blocks above the ground no longer fades in from the fog.
- **The item in an Alex skin's hand** in the right place (MC-72397).
- **Players and entities above y 256 or below 0** are no longer drawn black.
- **The world vanishing** with the camera above y 256 or below 0, looking level.
- **Fog that darkened** with the block at your feet (half slabs, stairs): the fog colour takes the light at your eyes.
- **Banners** frozen in old worlds.
- **A broken chest or sign** still drawn for a few frames.
- **Name tags flipped** in the front camera (F5 twice), as from 1.9.
- **The beacon beam** drawn twice, brighter than intended.
- **Heads** without transparency in the skin's hat layer.
- **Arrows, the fishing bobber and fire** on entities with a black or opaque background.
- **The pumpkin overlay** for players in spectator mode.

## Protection from servers

Packets made to freeze or crash the client are now refused:

- NBT with a huge size (an overflow in the size check);
- fake counts in explosions, chunks, block changes, removed entities and maps;
- millions of particles in a single packet;
- a potion effect with an id that does not exist;
- an item with an unknown id;
- a corrupt server icon;
- packets arriving after the disconnect, a player spawned before joining the tab list, and plugin messages.

## Less memory

Freed once used: deleted textures, map textures when changing worlds or servers, the main menu's background, the singleplayer server after leaving, the enchantment helper that kept the last world, and the redstone torch list of each world.

## Components

Stein Bridge 0.1.30, Stein Loader 0.1.34 and Stein Client 0.3.10 (each game session's log is also kept in `logs/stein-client/`).

If you prefer the old behaviour of any of these, each change has a `-Dsteinbridge.opt.vanilla…` option (listed in the Bridge's `docs/OPTIONS.md`).

To update, open the Stein Client or the installer again. Mods, options and worlds stay where they are.
