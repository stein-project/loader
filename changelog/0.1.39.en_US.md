# Stein Loader 0.1.39: smooth singleplayer at long render distances

Singleplayer no longer freezes when you play with a long render distance: new land loads a little at a time, so the world keeps moving and the FPS stays high. This version also brings a built-in benchmark, so you can measure Stein on your computer and send us the result.

- **Singleplayer without freezes**: at long render distances (32 chunks), mobs, damage and world generation no longer stop while new land loads. In our tests the FPS went from about 130 to over 600, and saving the world no longer pauses the game.
- **Smoother flight over new land**: the worst frames while flying over land that has just loaded went from around 100 ms to under 10 ms in our tests.
- **Built-in benchmark**: start the game with `-Dsteinbridge.bench=true` in the Java arguments (the Stein Client's developer options) and it plays a fixed scene on its own: a 400-player fight, mobs, a thunderstorm, every block, caves, the biomes, the Nether, the End and the menus. It tries every graphics mode your computer has and saves one file in the `stein-bench` folder for you to send us. Keyboard and mouse are off while it runs; close the window to stop it.
- **Components**: Stein Bridge 0.1.25 and Stein Loader 0.1.29.

To update, open the Stein Client or the installer again. Mods, options and worlds stay where they are.
