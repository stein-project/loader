# Stein Loader 0.1.27: Stein loading screen and new look

A new Stein loading screen, an optional new look and several lighting fixes.

- **Stein loading screen**: when the game starts, when textures change (Resource Packs, F3+T) and when graphics stall to load, the Stein screen with a progress bar shows up, in the style of the newer versions.
- **Graphics**: the graphics slider applies when you let go, not while dragging.
- **New look (optional)**: textures and sounds of the latest Minecraft in 1.8.9, built on your computer. Check it in the installer or turn it on in the Stein Client settings. "Programmer Art" in Resource Packs brings back the 1.8.9 look.
- **Lighting**: holding an enchanted item no longer turns chests purple or darkens the health and armor bars. With shaders, block sides are no longer cut in half by the shadow.
- **Connection**: fixed the "NullPointerException: group" error when joining a server directly.
- **Chat**: Ctrl no longer gets stuck (A selected everything).
- **Panel**: arrows only on options that pick a value, and the left one goes back.
- **Components**: Stein Bridge 0.1.16 and Stein Loader 0.1.20.

To update, open the Stein Client or the installer again. Mods, options and worlds stay where they are.
