# Stein Loader 0.1.45: the groundwork for Combat's new modules

The Loader gained what mods need to deal with items, sounds and potions, and the text a mod draws in the world shows up again.

- **Items, sounds and potions for mods**: a mod can now tell which item lies on the ground, play a game sound, stop the player's sprint and read a potion's effects and their duration, server custom potions included. Combat's new modules use them.
- **Text in the world**: labels a mod draws over something in the world used the HUD font and never showed; they now use the game's font, like the names above players.
- **Components**: Stein Bridge 0.1.29 and Stein Loader 0.1.33.

To update, open the Stein Client or the installer again. Mods, options and worlds stay where they are.
