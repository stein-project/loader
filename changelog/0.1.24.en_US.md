# Stein Loader 0.1.24: new HUD and performance defaults

New default settings for new players. If you already play, your settings stay as they are.

- **HUD ready out of the box**: armor, effects, direction, stats and inventory from the HUD come enabled and in place, with a cleaner boss bar and sidebar and the chat without a background.
- **Performance**: direct rendering to the window, the compact angle table and waiting for neighbouring chunks are now enabled by default.
- **Components**: Stein Bridge 0.1.13 and Stein Loader 0.1.17.

To update, open the installer again. Mods, options and worlds stay where they are.
