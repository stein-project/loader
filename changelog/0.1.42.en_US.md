# Stein Loader 0.1.42: the server decides, and the Panel tells you about updates

Stein stops forbidding mods. Each server can now ask to turn off a mod or a feature while you play there, and the Panel shows what is off.

- **The server decides**: a server can turn off a whole mod or just one feature. The Loader takes that mod's screen elements, Panel page and keys out, and everything comes back when you leave. Your choices don't change.
- **Careful on Hypixel and other servers**: Stein no longer turns anything off on its own on any server, Hypixel included. If a server doesn't ask, the mod stays on, and minding the rules of where you play is up to you.
- **Update notices in the Panel**: each mod shows when a new version is out. For now it is only a notice; the Stein Client or you still do the updating.
- **No more banned mods**: the Loader no longer blocks mods by a list.
- **Components**: Stein Bridge 0.1.27 and Stein Loader 0.1.31.

To update, open the Stein Client or the installer again. Mods, options and worlds stay where they are.
